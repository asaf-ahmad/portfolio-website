FAQ

Questions people ask before hiring

Who do you typically consult for?

Banks and NBFCs building or buying lending technology, fintechs designing credit products, lending-software vendors who need pre-sales or product help, and investors assessing a lending platform. Fractional product work and mentoring are not limited to fintech. Engagements have covered India, Sri Lanka, Bhutan and the Middle East.

What makes you different from a generalist product consultant?

I have built a loan origination system as an engineer, run five concurrent LOS implementations as a product manager, shipped five AI capabilities into live credit workflows, and closed a USD 1M+ deal myself. The advice comes from having done the work, not from a framework.

Can you help us decide whether to build AI in-house or buy it?

Yes. That decision is most of what AI-in-lending advisory is about. We look at the use case, the data you actually hold, the PII and regulatory exposure, the vendor landscape and your team's capacity, then write it up as a recommendation your risk and technology teams can both sign.

How is mentoring structured?

One-to-one sessions of forty-five to sixty minutes. Most people book three to five sessions over a couple of months. You bring the goal, I bring structure, feedback and a point of view from inside fintech product management.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. I am based in Mumbai and work remotely with clients across time zones. Onsite workshops can be arranged for discovery and pre-sales engagements in India and nearby markets.

How do fees work?

Advisory sessions are priced per session, sprints as a fixed fee and retainers monthly. Mentoring is priced per session with a discount for a series. Send the brief and I will reply with a quote after the discovery call.
