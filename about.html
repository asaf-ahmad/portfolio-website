Who I Am

I build fintech products that move money, reduce risk, and make credit accessible.

Four years of end-to-end product ownership across Loan Origination Systems, Digital Lending, MSME Credit Underwriting, Loan Collections, and AI-powered lending automation — for banks, NBFCs, and financial institutions across India, the Middle East, and South Asia.

My domain is the full lending lifecycle. From the moment a borrower applies to the moment a loan is disbursed, repaid, or recovered — I have built, standardised, and shipped products at every stage of that journey.

Before moving into product, I was a developer — which means I understand what I am asking engineers to build, and why that matters.

Consistent top performer across both companies. Three consecutive appraisals averaging thirty percent. Two consecutive Star of Quarter awards. Never had an average year.
