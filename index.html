Lending & AI Expertise

Deep product experience across the full lending lifecycle, from lead to disbursement to collections, with AI built into the workflow rather than bolted on.

Core Domain

AI in Lending

Own the AI product roadmap for an enterprise lending platform. Use-case discovery with credit and risk teams, requirement definition, prioritisation and adoption after go-live, with PII-risk handling designed into every component.

Core Domain

Loan Origination Systems

End-to-end LOS strategy, architecture and implementation. Three years building FinnOne Neo for a Middle Eastern bank, then five concurrent LOS roll-outs for global clients as a product manager.

Decisioning

Business Rule Engine

Configurable credit policy as rules, not code: eligibility and knock-out checks, scorecards, deviation matrices, approval hierarchies and pricing grids that business users maintain without a release cycle.

Credit

Automated credit appraisal, CAM generation and report generators with model formulation and parameterisation, built to cut manual decisioning time for credit teams.

Journeys

Onboarding
